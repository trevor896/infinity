Journey

How I Started

My coding journey began with curiosity. I started by learning HTML and CSS on YouTube, turning simple ideas into visible, functioning pages and discovering how design and structure could shape a digital experience.

As my confidence grew, I began exploring small data and analytics personal projects with the support of AI tools. That phase helped me understand the value of building quickly, experimenting boldly, and letting creativity guide the process.

Even so, the path was not smooth. After many trials, setbacks, and moments where progress felt invisible, I chose to reset and approach learning with more discipline. I turned to freeCodeCamp, where I rebuilt my fundamentals, strengthened my understanding, and gained a stronger sense of structure in both code and thinking.

Over the past two years, I have grown comfortable working across both worlds: the technical side of building and the design side of shaping meaningful user experiences. Through Canva and Figma, I have learned to translate ideas into clarity, while the lessons from my failures have become some of my most valuable teachers.
